# Workflows & Scheduling

Rosmarium COS provides a robust workflow automation engine designed to support complex editorial approval processes and delayed publication.

## Workflow State Machine

Workflows in Rosmarium are powered by a state machine that allows you to define custom approval chains tailored to your team's needs.
Each Content Type can be assigned a default Workflow. 

- **States**: Define logical stages for your content (e.g., `Draft`, `In Review`, `Approved`, `Published`).
- **Transitions**: Define how content moves between states. 
- **Validations**: Transitions can be guarded by role-based access control or conditional rules to ensure that only authorized personnel can approve content.

### Visual Workflow Builder

The Admin Dashboard provides an intuitive, drag-and-drop ReactFlow-powered **Workflow Builder**. You can visually map out states and transitions, making it easy to design complex editorial processes without writing code.

![Workflow Builder](/docs/admin_workflow_builder.png)

### Workflow Timeline

Every transition is tracked in the database, creating a comprehensive audit log. The **Workflow Timeline** in the Content Editor displays exactly who made a transition, when it happened, and any comments they left during the review.

## Scheduled Publishing

Editorial teams often need to embargo content until a specific date and time. 

Rosmarium integrates with **BullMQ** (powered by Redis) to offer delayed publishing capabilities directly within the Content Editor.
- **Schedule to Publish**: Pick a future date and time for content to automatically become visible.
- **Schedule to Unpublish**: Automatically archive seasonal or time-sensitive content.

The system handles everything in the background reliably, ensuring your content is delivered precisely when intended.
