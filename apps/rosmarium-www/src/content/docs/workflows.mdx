# Workflows & Scheduling

Ensure brand consistency and compliance across your entire organization. Rosmarium COS provides a robust workflow automation engine designed to support complex enterprise editorial processes, legal approvals, and automated campaign launches.

## Enterprise State Machine

Workflows in Rosmarium are powered by a state machine that allows you to define custom approval chains tailored to your exact business hierarchy.

    <h4 class="text-white font-bold mb-1">States</h4>
    <p class="text-zinc-400 text-xs leading-relaxed">Define logical stages for your content (e.g., Draft, In Review, Published).</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="bg-dark-surface border border-dark-border p-5 rounded-lg text-center hover:border-neon-cyan transition-colors">
    <div class="text-neon-cyan text-2xl mb-2">🔄</div>
    <h4 class="text-white font-bold mb-1">Transitions</h4>
    <p class="text-zinc-400 text-xs leading-relaxed">Map exact directional paths defining how content moves between states.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="bg-dark-surface border border-dark-border p-5 rounded-lg text-center hover:border-neon-cyan transition-colors">
    <div class="text-neon-cyan text-2xl mb-2">🛡</div>
    <h4 class="text-white font-bold mb-1">Validations (RBAC)</h4>
    <p class="text-zinc-400 text-xs leading-relaxed">Guard transitions with rules to ensure only authorized personnel can approve content.</p>
  </div>
</div>

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Audit Trails:** Every transition requires a mandatory reason or comment, ensuring compliance and clear accountability across large organizations. Every transition is tracked in the database, creating a comprehensive audit log visible in the editor's **Workflow Timeline**.

## Scheduled Publishing

Editorial teams often need to embargo content until a specific date and time. Rosmarium integrates with **BullMQ** (powered by Redis) to offer delayed publishing capabilities directly within the Content Editor.
